## Delivery implementation boundary

The Delivery PR replaces blank/manual proposal authoring with deterministic proposals from explicit approved requirement lines. Supported lines use a stable requirement identifier (`REQ`, `BR`, `FR`, `NFR` or `AC`) or an explicit normative predicate (`must`, `shall`, `required to` or `needs to`). Headings and descriptive prose do not produce work. Epics summarize actual deliverables rather than document headings; stories separate the concrete deliverable from its source statement. An `AC` line is paired only when it explicitly names the requirement ID or when one requirement makes its same-section association unambiguous. A document without explicit requirements is blocked with an actionable message instead of producing empty or fabricated work.

The user reviews every proposal, edits its type/title/description/criteria, and selects a subset before publication. Requirement links and the generated proposal role remain immutable. The project-wide unique origin is the stable artifact aggregate plus immutable proposal role and canonically sorted section/requirement identities. Mutable work type is excluded from identity. Generated roles retain their pre-upgrade default-type hash domains (coordination/Epic, implementation/Story and verification/Task), so an unchanged proposal cannot be re-imported after upgrade under a new hash.
