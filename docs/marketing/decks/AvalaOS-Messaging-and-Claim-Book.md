# AvalaOS Messaging and Claim Book

## Core message

**AvalaOS is the governed decision and delivery layer between process discovery and execution.**

It helps enterprises decide what should be automated, how it should be automated, what must remain human-owned, and how the approved decision becomes traceable delivery work.

Canonical tagline: **Evaluate before you automate. Govern before you execute.**

## Audience messages

### Executive and transformation leader

The decision risk appears before development begins. AvalaOS makes the work, evidence, recommendation, controls, accountable approval, and delivery handoff legible before budget is committed.

### Automation / AI CoE

Standardize the contract for every candidate: evidence, versioned deterministic recommendation, human review, approved artifact, governed handoff, and portfolio visibility.

### Process Excellence and Business Analysis

Work with familiar process catalogs, Decision Packs, BRDs, FRDs, PDDs, work items, and review states while retaining source lineage and explicit assumptions.

### Risk and Control

Keep material-risk decisions human-owned. Record evidence gaps, approval conditions, action-specific controls, and blocked actions without pretending a control-plane record is runtime enforcement.

### Delivery leadership

Receive approved source context, acceptance rationale, ownership, blockers, and Delivery Pack lineage without asking the delivery team to reconstruct why the work exists.

### Investor

AvalaOS begins with automation decision intelligence and expands along the governed context into requirements, delivery, application modernization, and—only as a roadmap vision—governed software assembly.

## Technology-fit language

- Rules/workflow for stable, structured, deterministic work.
- RPA for repetitive UI work when a stronger interface is unavailable.
- AI assistance for unstructured extraction, classification, and summarisation.
- Bounded agent assistance for context-heavy investigation and evidence synthesis.
- Human/HITL for material-risk judgment and accountable approval.
- Hybrid operating model for mixed end-to-end processes.

The categories are composable. AvalaOS evaluates fit and controls; authorized systems execute.

## Current capability language

- Deterministic and versioned recommendation.
- Evidence-qualified confidence.
- Human-owned material-risk review and approval.
- Common enterprise artifacts such as BRD, FRD, and PDD.
- One governed handoff into Avala Delivery.
- Recorded outcomes or an explicit Not recorded state in Monitor.
- BYOK-ready architecture; provider availability is deployment-dependent.
- Source/CI/browser verification; hosted/live validation not run.
- Synthetic product preview. No live execution.

## Roadmap language

**Avala Assemble is a roadmap vision, not a current module or application-generation capability.** It would begin with approved evidence, requirements, controls, and lineage, then govern the composition of reusable forms, workflows, extraction, validations, approvals, connectors, reporting, and later-approved agent tools.

## Prohibited language

- AI decides scores, recommendations, risk gates, or approvals.
- Autonomous execution, autonomous delivery, one-click automation, or one-click delivery.
- Jira replacement or unproven connectors/synchronisation.
- Zero learning curve, no training, or instant adoption.
- Production-ready, hosted-ready, tenant-safe, certified, or compliant.
- Guaranteed ROI, savings, payback, or scientifically validated scoring.
- Live CMDB/repository/network scanning or autonomous modernization.
- Current white-label, reseller, partner, customer, revenue, or traction claims.
- Real-time Monitor telemetry.
- Studio private files/downloads as accepted current availability.
- Synthetic UI values as customer outcomes or commercial proof.
