# AvalaOS deck visual system

## Communication jobs

- **Executive overview:** by the end, automation and transformation leaders should understand why decision quality must precede tool choice and choose to explore one governed assessment.
- **Client transformation:** by the end, a buyer should choose one bounded assessment engagement because AvalaOS preserves evidence and accountability into delivery handoff.
- **Investor:** by the end, investors should see a focused commercial wedge and rigorous product foundation while understanding that deployment and commercial validation are the next milestones.
- **Brand evolution:** by the end, internal reviewers should select an evolutionary Avala Assemble identity direction without changing the current production brand.

## Typography

- Headings and large statements: Outfit, 600-700.
- Body and operational copy: Inter, 400-700.
- Deck titles: 44-54 pt depending on composition.
- Slide titles: 30-36 pt.
- Subheads and callouts: 20-26 pt.
- Body: 16-20 pt.
- Supporting note: 12-14 pt.
- Footer: 10-11 pt.

The generator references bundled Outfit and Inter font assets. A system-safe Aptos fallback is retained for environments that do not load the packaged fonts.

## Composition

- 16:9, 1280x720.
- 64 px outside margins and a consistent 8 px spacing rhythm.
- One central claim and one visual argument per slide.
- Flat compositions with restrained panels; avoid dense dashboard grids.
- Product screenshots remain large enough to read, with a visible synthetic-preview qualifier.
- Amber is a decision/action accent, not a background color.
- Dark trust bands use navy with thin amber rules and pale text.
- Investor slides use more dark canvas and sharper contrast; client slides use light operational canvas; marketing alternates bold statement and screenshot-led pages.

## Reusable visual arguments

1. Technology-fit spectrum: six work types converging into a hybrid operating model.
2. Governed lifecycle: Assess -> Govern -> Studio -> Delivery -> Monitor.
3. Evidence spine: evidence -> deterministic decision -> human governance -> artifact -> handoff -> visibility.
4. Current / candidate / roadmap status bands.

## Screenshot rules

- Use only committed assets under `public/marketing/screenshots/`.
- Every use is labelled `Synthetic product preview. No live execution.`
- Do not interpret fixture counts, dates, percentages, plan labels, names, or outcome signals as product performance or traction.
- The Application Portfolio capture shows a legacy V1 AP Invoice Decision Pack above the fold and cannot be the sole visual proof of the portfolio workspace.
- The Studio capture shows read-only maintenance and must not imply active mutation.

## Brand-board rules

- Preserve the AvalaOS name, wordmark proportions, current A silhouette, navy/amber equity, and premium enterprise tone.
- Concepts are internal exploratory marks, not production replacements or finished guidelines.
- Recommend the Assembly Node because it introduces the future joining metaphor with the least loss of existing recognition.
