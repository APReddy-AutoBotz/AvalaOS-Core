# AvalaOS Core narrative foundation

## Positioning

AvalaOS is the governed decision and delivery layer between process discovery and execution. It helps enterprises decide what should be automated, how it should be automated, what must remain human-owned, and how the approved decision becomes traceable delivery work.

Canonical tagline: **Evaluate before you automate. Govern before you execute.**

Primary problem: enterprises do not lack automation tools. They lack a governed decision system for choosing the right mechanism, proving the decision, controlling material risk, and carrying approved context into delivery.

Executive question: **What should be automated, by which mechanism, under what controls, with what evidence, and who remains accountable?**

## Narrative spine

`Evidence -> deterministic decision -> human governance -> governed artifact -> delivery handoff -> outcome visibility`

The product lifecycle is `Assess -> Govern -> Studio -> Delivery -> Monitor`, with Admin and Trust providing organization, provider, evidence, audit, and proof-status surfaces.

## Technology-fit story

A business process is usually a portfolio of work types, not one technology choice:

- Stable, structured, deterministic work can fit rules or workflow.
- Repetitive work across user interfaces can fit RPA where a stronger interface is unavailable.
- Unstructured extraction, classification, or summarisation can fit AI-assisted automation.
- Context-heavy investigation and evidence synthesis can fit bounded agent-assisted work.
- Material-risk decisions and accountable judgement remain human or human-in-the-loop.
- Mixed processes require a governed hybrid operating model.

AvalaOS decomposes the process, evaluates evidence, applies versioned deterministic decision logic, identifies controls and review needs, and prepares a governed handoff. Authorized workflow, RPA, agent, and delivery systems execute the work.

## Module truth

- **Avala Assess:** process intake, primitive-level decomposition, evidence-qualified confidence, deterministic recommendation, Decision Packs, scenario economics where supported, and Application Portfolio assessment.
- **Avala Govern:** independent review, assumptions and evidence gaps, material-risk controls, action-specific governance conditions, human approval, and handoff readiness. It is a control plane, not an execution runtime.
- **Avala Studio:** canonical structured BRD, FRD, and PDD artifacts with immutable revisions, independent review, separate approval, and exact source lineage. Private Markdown/PDF/DOCX renditions and brokered downloads remain a Studio PR B implementation candidate.
- **Avala Delivery:** a governed workbench for approved work, owners, blockers, source lineage, and Delivery Packs. It complements execution systems and is not a Jira replacement.
- **Avala Monitor:** a read-only product view of disposition, readiness, risk, blockers, lineage, and recorded or absent outcomes. It does not create tasks or claim live runtime telemetry.
- **Avala Admin / Trust / AI Controls:** real read-only product surfaces for organization context, provider direction, proof status, limitations, evidence, and audit. Later server read models, administrative controls, and entitlements remain roadmap work.

Correct handoff language: **One governed handoff converts approved context into source-linked work items in Avala Delivery. Avala Monitor then provides readiness, risk, blocker, lineage, value, and outcome visibility.**

## Adoption language

AvalaOS is designed around familiar enterprise roles and artifacts—process catalogs, assessments, reviews, Decision Packs, BRDs, FRDs, PDDs, work items, Delivery Packs, and portfolio views—reducing adoption friction without forcing teams to replace their execution ecosystem.

Never claim zero learning curve, no training, instant adoption, one-click automation, or autonomous delivery.

## Proof boundary

Accepted source and CI authority covers deterministic Assess through Application Portfolio, independent review and Govern handoff, and canonical Studio BRD/FRD/PDD artifacts. Studio private artifacts are candidate-only. Hosted, deployment, pilot, production, buyer, release-candidate, security-certification, and compliance readiness remain unproven.

Every committed product screenshot is a synthetic 1440x900 capture. UI counts, names, dates, percentages, plans, and outcome signals are fixtures, not customer or commercial evidence.
