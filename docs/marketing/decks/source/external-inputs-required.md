# External inputs required

The generated decks remain polished discussion drafts when these values are absent. Missing values are recorded in speaker notes and are never exposed as visible `TODO` boxes.

## Company and contact

- Legal entity.
- Approved website and contact email.
- Confidentiality label.
- Approved public CTA or data-room route.

## Founder and team

- Founder name and title.
- Verifiable biography, relevant experience, and awards.
- Current team and hiring plan.

## Commercial model

- Approved license range and terms.
- Implementation and enablement range.
- Customisation and enhancement policy.
- Support and update agreement.
- Hosted pricing hypothesis and target gross margin.
- White-label rights, branding model, reseller boundaries, territory, retained AvalaOS IP, and enhancement ownership.

## Commercial evidence

- Current revenue, customers, pilots, design partners, pipeline, and evidence for each, if any.
- Validated customer interviews and willingness-to-pay evidence.
- Approved buyer references, outcomes, or case studies. The AP Invoice Exception journey remains synthetic until a real reference is approved.

## Fundraise

- Round and raise amount.
- Runway target and target close.
- Use of funds.
- Legal entity and cap-table/diligence details appropriate for the deck.

## Market research pass

A later evidence-backed market-sizing pass requires:

- Approved geography and ideal-customer-profile definitions.
- Category boundaries that avoid double counting process mining, governance, delivery, and execution markets.
- Dated primary or reputable research sources.
- Firm counts and spend basis.
- Reproducible TAM, SAM, and SOM methodology.
- Approved CAGR or market-growth sources, if used.

No market size, CAGR, pricing, margin, revenue, customer, partnership, pipeline, conversion, valuation, or runway number is inferred in the current suite.

## Client configuration

- Client name, industry, logo, selected process, and engagement owner.
- Named process owner and control reviewer.
- Evidence available for the selected process.
- Client-specific security, deployment, and integration requirements.

## Legal and brand

- Trademark and legal review for `Avala Assemble`.
- Approval for any external use of the Assess / Validate / Assemble / Launch / Audit expansion.
- Approval before any concept replaces or modifies a production asset.
