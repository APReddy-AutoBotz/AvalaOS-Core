# Consulting Partner / White-label module

Recommended insertion after client core slide 12.

## CP1 — Turn discovery into a repeatable, evidence-backed client method

- Reusable assessment structure.
- Client-specific evidence and Decision Pack.
- Governed BRD/FRD/PDD artifact package.
- Delivery-handoff preparation.
- Repeatable review and control language.

Qualifier: this is a proposed partner operating model. It is not proof of current white-label platform behaviour, reseller rights, or client-branding capability.

## CP2 — Define the partner model before making a commercial promise

- Partner: client relationship, discovery, implementation, enablement, and approved customisation.
- AvalaOS: platform IP, deterministic decision law, product releases, and platform governance boundary.
- Joint: change control, support responsibilities, evidence ownership, and client approval.

AP and legal inputs required: licensing structure, reseller or territory terms, branding rules, implementation and support scope, enhancement ownership, pricing, and retained IP language.
