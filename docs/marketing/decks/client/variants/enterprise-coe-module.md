# Enterprise Automation / AI CoE module

Recommended insertion after client core slide 12 as an alternative to the Consulting Partner module.

## CoE1 — Build portfolio discipline before technology becomes the default

Inputs: process inventory, application inventory, evidence, owners, and constraints.

Outputs: governed dispositions, application-readiness bands, dependency waves, risk and control gaps, and evidence-qualified economics ranges.

## CoE2 — Give every initiative the same decision-to-handoff contract

- Evidence and assumption taxonomy.
- Versioned deterministic recommendation.
- Human review and action-specific control resolution.
- Approved artifact package.
- Source-linked delivery handoff.
- Executive readiness, blocker, lineage, and outcome visibility.

Do not imply live enterprise discovery, CMDB scanning, automatic roadmap approval, direct delivery-tool synchronisation, or operational telemetry.
